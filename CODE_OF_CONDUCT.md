# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
adapted to a small maintainer-led project.

## Our pledge

We want participation in GridScalr to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience, background, identity, or the size of their
contribution. Measuring drawings is precision work; the same care applies to how
we talk to each other.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and technical. Critique the code, the design or the measurement
  logic, not the person.
- Assume good faith and ask before rewriting someone else's work.
- Respect that the maintainer decides scope, especially the local-first
  guarantees: no accounts, cloud sync, telemetry or network dependencies.
- Do not attach customer drawings, personal data or credentials to issues or
  pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Dismissing reports of a problem because you cannot reproduce it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile
[@MajorJohnDoe](https://github.com/MajorJohnDoe), or through the private
advisory flow in `SECURITY.md` if the report is sensitive.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts.
Consequences escalate from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban,
depending on severity and repetition. Reports are handled confidentially.
