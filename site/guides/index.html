Guides for the work on the drawing

Short, practical guides for the three jobs: measure areas, draw operation cards and take off order quantities. Every example uses the same demo project that ships with the app.

The term, who does it and what the quantities become. 5 min What is an operation card? The HVAC document, its fields and who uses it. 5 min Operation card fields Property, system, date, signature, page and revision. 6 min Ventilation operation card Air handling unit, ducts, sensors and a clean PDF export. 6 min Measure a scanned TIF drawing Import without conversion, lossless scans and the cache. 5 min Compare two drawing revisions Read-only side-by-side, overlay and comparison PDF. 5 min Mark a deviation on a drawing Cloud, callout, stamp and an export for the contractor. 4 min Send a drawing by e-mail The export dialog, then an unsent draft with the PDF attached. 5 min GridScalr and Bluebeam An honest comparison, and when Bluebeam is the better choice. 5 min GridScalr and Excel When a spreadsheet is enough, and when traceability wins.
