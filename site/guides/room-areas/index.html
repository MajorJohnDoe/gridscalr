Measure a room area and link it to the room

The drawing holds the measurement. The register holds the room. Linking them is what makes the area reviewable.

Updated 24 November 2026 · 5 min read · Checked against GridScalr 0.32.0

Short answer

Draw the room as an area measurement, open View → Spaces… and link the measurement to the room. A measured room sums its visible linked areas minus deductions, while a manual value is only a fallback. A room without a usable area shows Missing instead of a partial sum.

Build the register first

The space register at View → Spaces… is a tree: a building contains floors, and a floor contains rooms or zones. Each room has a room number, a name, a floor, a usage and a status, and can optionally be linked to one drawing page. The structure is what lets you read areas per floor and per building instead of one flat list.

Create rooms from the room text starts from the room names already on the measurements. It creates root-level rooms and never links a measurement, so move them under the right building and floor as a separate, deliberate step.

Measured or manual area

Measured from linked area measurements sums the visible area measurements linked to the space: additions minus deductions, converted to m². Manual area stores a value you type. The area is never stored twice, and a linked measurement always wins over a manual value.

The manual value is a fallback for a room that cannot be measured, for example a corridor outside the drawing set. When a measurement is linked, the row reports that the manual value is not used, so nobody has to guess which figure is current.

Link the measurements

Select one or more area measurements on the drawing, open View → Spaces… and use the link action on the room row. The hint under the buttons names how many area measurements are selected. Only area measurements can be linked, and a locked measurement cannot be linked.

A link moves the measurement into the room; linking it to another room moves it again. The search action on a room selects its first visible linked measurement and zooms to it, which is the fastest way to check that the right polygon belongs to the right room.

Read the warnings

Missing means the room has no usable area: nothing is linked, or a linked measurement has no page scale. GridScalr never falls back to a manual value on its own and never shows a partial sum as if it were complete. A missing area is a prompt to measure, link or set a manual fallback, not a number to ignore.

Hidden measurements and hidden layers are excluded from the sum, while locked measurements are included. That matches the quantity report and every export. A deduction applies inside its own category and room, so a shaft linked to the wrong room leaves both rooms wrong. The register always reports m², even when the project uses another area unit for measurements.

Check per floor and building

The Rooms and Area cards read the space register, so a floor with a missing room shows up as a named warning rather than a zero. Work from the building down: every floor should have its rooms, every measured room should have at least one linked area, and every manual value should have a reason.

The register is a dialog opened from View → Spaces…, not a dock panel. Link the measurements first and reopen the register after a selection change, because the dialog does not rebuild itself when the selection behind it changes.

Common mistakes

  • Linking a deduction to a different room from its gross area.
  • Setting a manual area and expecting it to update when a measurement is linked later.
  • Leaving a room as Missing and treating it as zero in the total.
  • Creating rooms from room text and assuming the measurements are linked automatically.

Questions

Does a linked measurement update the room area?

Yes. The room sums its visible linked area measurements, additions minus deductions, and recalculates when a linked measurement changes.

What does Missing mean?

The room has no usable area: nothing is linked, or a linked measurement has no page scale. GridScalr shows the warning instead of a partial sum.

Can I type an area instead of measuring?

Yes, as a manual area. It is a fallback while nothing is linked; as soon as an area measurement is linked, the measured value wins.
